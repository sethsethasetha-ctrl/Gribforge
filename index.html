GRIBFORGE

Match parts. Build a goblin crew. Hold the line on iPhone Duo.

Gribforge is an offline, landscape strategy game for iPhone Duo, iPhone, and iPad. Match mechanical parts, deploy a goblin crew, and defend a scrappy workshop with improvised war machines.

Play seamlessly on iPhone Duo’s outer screen and spacious inner screen, with a responsive layout that keeps the board, lanes, and controls readable.

Build your battle line

Match to deploy

Swipe a 10 × 8 board to match Wrenches, Coil Rifles, Blast Cores, and Assault Helmets. Larger matches create stronger units.

Three-lane defense

Watch your goblins advance, target enemies, and protect your base across three active lanes.

Assemble a war machine

Collect parts, choose three candidates in Assembly Carnival, and send a crafted machine back into the fight.

Made for iPhone Duo

Play in landscape on both Duo displays. The scene rebuilds its layout when the screen changes, keeping the match board, battle lanes, and controls in reach.

Play offline

Thirty levels, local progress, and optional music and sound effects with no account or network connection required.
