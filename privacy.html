Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

Zichen Play Studio (“we,” “us,” or “our”) provides NOCLINE. This Privacy Policy explains how NOCLINE handles information when you use the app.

Information We Handle

NOCLINE stores gameplay progress, unlocked relays, star results, and your music, haptics, and rhythm-assist preferences locally on your device. This information is used only to make the app work and to preserve your settings.

The app may process a device identifier for app functionality. It is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking, advertising, profiling, or cross-app activity.

What We Do Not Collect

NOCLINE does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos, microphone input, payment information, or user-generated content. The app does not include third-party advertising, behavioral analytics, or data sale.

Sharing and Tracking

We do not sell or share personal information with third parties. NOCLINE does not track you across apps or websites, and it does not use your information for targeted advertising.

Data Retention and Your Choices

Local gameplay data remains on your device until you delete the app or clear the app’s data through your device settings. You can change music, haptics, and rhythm-assist preferences inside the app.

Children’s Privacy

NOCLINE is not designed to collect personal information from children. Because the app does not request personal information or provide user accounts, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app’s data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when a revised policy is published.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Zichen Play Studio using the developer contact details shown on the NOCLINE App Store product page.
