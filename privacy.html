Gribforge Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

Gribforge is an offline game published by Bao Zi Chen Play Studio. This Privacy Policy explains what information the game handles when you use it.

Information we collect

Gribforge does not require an account and does not collect names, email addresses, precise location, contacts, photos, payment information, or other personal information. The game does not include advertising, analytics, social sign-in, or a remote server connection.

Information stored on your device

The game stores local gameplay preferences and progress using Apple’s standard local storage APIs. This includes unlocked levels and the music and sound-effect settings. This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Audio

The game plays bundled music and sound effects through Apple’s audio frameworks. It does not record, upload, or analyze microphone or camera data.

Children’s privacy

Because Gribforge does not collect personal information or use behavioral advertising, it is designed to minimize data collection for players of all ages. Parents or guardians can contact the publisher through the support channel listed on the App Store product page with questions.

Third-party services

Gribforge does not integrate third-party advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or account services.

Changes to this policy

If the game’s data practices change, this page will be updated with a new effective date. Continued use of the game after an update means the revised policy applies.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the support contact shown on the Gribforge App Store listing.
